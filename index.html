About Dr de Villiers

Where medicine meets aesthetics

I am a registered medical doctor with the Irish Medical Council (IMC 413939), practising in Ireland since 2015. After seven fulfilling years as a General Practitioner, I transitioned into aesthetic medicine in 2021 following specialised training at Cosmed Academy in Dublin.

Since then, I have treated thousands of patients using neurotoxins, dermal fillers, biostimulators and medical-grade skincare. My approach combines my extensive medical background with aesthetic expertise to create tailored treatment plans focused on natural, balanced results and healthy ageing.

Every treatment is delivered according to current Irish medical and safety standards, with your wellbeing always at the centre of everything I do.

Questions

Good to know

How do I book?

Send a request using the form below with your preferred date and time of day. Dr de Villiers replies personally to confirm your appointment. You can also message on WhatsApp or call if you prefer.

Do I need a consultation first?

Yes. Every treatment plan begins with a consultation, so your goals, medical history and options are discussed before anything is done. There is no obligation to proceed.

Will I look overdone?

No. The aim is to enhance your natural features, not change them. You will get honest advice, including when a treatment is not the right choice for you.

How much do treatments cost?

Cost depends on the treatment and your individual plan. You will be given clear pricing when your appointment is confirmed or at your consultation, before any treatment goes ahead.

How long do results last?

It varies by treatment and by person. Your expected results, timelines and any follow-up are explained at your consultation.

Where are you based?

In Ballincollig, Co. Cork. The full address and directions are sent with your appointment confirmation.
