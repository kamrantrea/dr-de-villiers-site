Where medicine meets aesthetics

I am a registered medical doctor with the Irish Medical Council (IMC 413939), practising in Ireland since 2015. After seven fulfilling years as a General Practitioner, I transitioned into aesthetic medicine in 2021 following specialised training at Cosmed Academy in Dublin.

Since then, I have treated thousands of patients using neurotoxins, dermal fillers, biostimulators and medical-grade skincare. My approach combines my extensive medical background with aesthetic expertise to create tailored treatment plans focused on natural, balanced results and healthy ageing.

Every treatment is delivered according to current Irish medical and safety standards, with your wellbeing always at the centre of everything I do.
