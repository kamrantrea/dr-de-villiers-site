Privacy policy

Dr Chrisna De Villiers ("I", "my") respects your privacy. This page explains how personal data collected through this website is used.

What I collect

If you use the appointment request form, I collect your name, email, phone number, the treatment you are interested in, your preferred times, and any message you choose to write. Please do not include medical details in the form.

Why and how it is used

Your details are used only to reply to your enquiry and arrange an appointment. The legal basis is your consent and taking steps at your request before a treatment. Medical history and consent forms are collected separately and securely during your consultation, not through this website.

Who receives it

Form submissions are delivered to my email through a form-processing service (Web3Forms). I do not sell or share your data for marketing.

How long I keep it

Enquiry emails are kept only as long as needed to respond and arrange care, and are then deleted unless you become a patient, in which case clinical records are held according to Irish medical record-keeping requirements.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete your data, or withdraw consent at any time, by emailing me. You also have the right to complain to the Data Protection Commission (dataprotection.ie).
